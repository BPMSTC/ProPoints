📚 Assignment, Project & Exam Policy

📅 Due Dates

All assignments are due on the assigned date. If you cannot attend class or submit on time, you must contact the instructor to arrange an alternative submission method.

⏰ Late Submission Policy

Standard assignments, labs, and quizzes can be submitted up to three days late with no penalty.

  • Up to 3 days late: No penalty
  • 4-10 days late: 5% penalty per day beyond the 3rd day
    Example: Submitting 5 days late = 10% penalty (2 days × 5% penalty)
  • After 10 days: No credit will be given

🖥️ In-Class Labs

  • Due the same day they are assigned
  • Labs submitted after the day ends will receive no credit unless prior arrangements are made with the instructor

📹Remote Attendance Policy

If attending remotely, failing to turn on your camera without a valid reason will result in a 20% penalty on in-class work.

⚠️ This is your FIRST and LAST warning for the semester. No additional reminders will be given.

📋 Important Notes

  • Some labs and assignments must be completed before moving on to later learning plans
  • When in doubt about deadlines or policies, contact the instructor immediately
  • Plan ahead and manage your time effectively to avoid penalties

💡 Success Tip: Set personal deadlines 1-2 days before official due dates to avoid last-minute issues!
