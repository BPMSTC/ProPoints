Student Computer Specifications Guide

Important Note

These specifications are designed to ensure a smooth academic experience. While the acceptable specs will meet basic needs, recommended specs provide additional performance headroom for more demanding tasks and future software requirements.

Acceptable Specifications

Processor (CPU): Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 (4 cores, 8 threads)
Memory (RAM): 16 GB DDR4
Storage: 256 GB SSD (Solid State Drive)
Graphics: Integrated GPU (Intel UHD Graphics or AMD Radeon Vega)
Display: 14-inch to 15.6-inch Full HD (1920x1080)
Ports: USB 3.0, USB-C, HDMI, Ethernet port (if available)
Battery Life: At least 6 hours
Networking: Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0

Recommended Specifications

Processor (CPU): Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 (6-8 cores, 12-16 threads)
Memory (RAM): 32 GB DDR4 or DDR5
Storage: 512 GB or 1 TB NVMe SSD
Graphics: Dedicated GPU (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 or AMD Radeon RX 5500M)
Display: 15.6-inch to 17.3-inch Full HD or QHD (2560x1440) with high color accuracy
Ports: Multiple USB 3.0, USB-C, HDMI, Ethernet, SD card reader
Battery Life: At least 8 hours
Networking: Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.2
Other: Backlit keyboard, good cooling system, and reliable build quality
