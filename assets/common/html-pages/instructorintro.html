Meet Your Instructor: Brent Presley

Hello, My Name is Brent Presley

I'd like to tell you a little bit about me so that you know who is teaching you this semester :)

I have been an instructor at Mid-State since 2014 teaching Software Development courses. During the time I've been an instructor, I've taught nearly every course in the program at one time. Over the years, I've seen many students succeed, and helping students achieve their goals is something I truly enjoy. I believe that my job is to teach students how to be successful as software developers, and that means helping you develop habits that will make you a lifetime learner.

My Background

My career has spanned software development and management, with experience in fields such as fast food, sales, insurance claims, consulting, and education. I think that this background helps me provide a unique perspective on what a company needs from software developers. This perspective also comes along with some stories that I tell during class from time to time.

A Lifelong Learner

I am a lifelong learner, and the field of software development is perfect for that reason. In addition to learning on my own, I hold three Bachelor's Degrees and two Master's Degrees. In short, I've spent a lot of time as a student, so I think I understand what you are going through in my class. If you ever have suggestions, please let me know!

Looking Forward

I look forward to working with you and helping you grow into a successful software developer. Let's make this a great semester!
